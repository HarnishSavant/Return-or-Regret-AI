# E-Commerce Return & Refund Policy

## 1. General Return Window
Customers can initiate a return or exchange within 30 days of item delivery. Items must be in unworn, unwashed condition with all original tags attached and original packaging intact.

## 2. Avoidable Returns and Fit Exchanges
We actively encourage exchanges for sizing rather than full refunds.
- **Free Size Exchanges:** If an item does not fit comfortably, size exchanges within the same product style are 100% free with pre-paid return shipping.
- **No Penalty Guarantee:** Customers are never penalized or restricted in future shopping privileges for making legitimate returns or sizing exchanges.

## 3. Product Exceptions
- **Footwear:** Must be tried on carpeted surfaces only. Scuffed soles or creased leather uppers are ineligible for full refund.
- **Undergarments & Activewear:** Compression shorts and sports bras must be tried on over existing clean undergarments with protective hygiene strips intact.
- **Final Sale:** Clearance items discounted at 40% or higher marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned unless manufacturing defects are present.

## 4. Refund Processing Timeframes
- Once received at our fulfillment center, items undergo inspection within 48 to 72 hours.
- Refunds are issued back to the original payment method within 3-5 business days of inspection approval.
- Store credits are credited immediately with an extra 10% bonus incentive.

## 5. Bracketing Policy
If you are uncertain between two consecutive sizes, please consult the AI Shopping Assistant first to inspect actual garment dimensions before ordering multiple sizes.
