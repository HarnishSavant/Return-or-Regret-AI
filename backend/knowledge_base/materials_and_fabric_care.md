# Materials, Fabrics & Dimensional Stability Guide

## 1. Natural Fibers
- **Egyptian & Supima Long-Staple Cotton:** High durability, exceptional breathability, silky smooth drape. Pre-shrunk during finishing, but washing at hot temperatures (>40°C) can cause 2-3% shrinkage in length.
- **Mulberry Silk:** Luxurious hand-feel, hypoallergenic, zero mechanical elasticity. Bias-cut garments cling to body heat and natural curves. Dry clean recommended.
- **Merino Wool & Cashmere:** Natural temperature regulation, odor-resistant, excellent drape. Must be laid flat to dry; hanging causes vertical shoulder elongation.
- **French Flax Linen:** Inelastic natural fiber that softens with wash. Known to feel stiff on first wear. Has zero give at waistbands, so exact sizing is critical.

## 2. Technical Synthetics & Elastomeric Blends
- **Elastane / Spandex (2-5% blends):** Adds lateral give and recovery. Eliminates waistband pinching in selvedge denim and chinos.
- **Recycled Polyamide / Lycra Xtra Life (Compression):** Delivers 4-way elastic memory. Leggings will feel tight during first step-in but conform to musculature within 10 minutes of body heat.
- **3-Ply Ripstop Nylon (Waterproof DWR):** Shell fabric does not stretch. Designed with anatomical articulated elbows and room for insulating midlayers.

## 3. Care Rules to Avoid Accidental Shrinkage Returns
Always air dry 100% cotton garments to prevent inseam and sleeve shrinkage. High heat tumble drying is responsible for 18% of customer-reported "size reduction" complaints.
