# Sizing & Body Measurement Master Guide

## 1. How to Take Accurate Body Measurements
Use a flexible fabric measuring tape. Keep the tape level, comfortably snug, but not digging into your skin.

### Upper Body (Shirts, T-Shirts, Outerwear)
- **Chest / Bust:** Measure around the fullest part of the chest/bust, keeping the tape horizontal under the armpits and across shoulder blades.
- **Shoulder Width:** Measure from the outer edge of one shoulder bone across the natural curve of the upper back to the opposite shoulder bone.
- **Sleeve Length:** From the center back of the neck, across the shoulder point, down to the wrist bone.
- **Collar / Neck:** Around the base of the neck where a shirt collar naturally sits, allowing two fingers of breathing room.

### Lower Body (Trousers, Jeans, Chinos)
- **Natural Waist:** Measure around the narrowest circumference between bottom of ribcage and navel (usually 1-2 inches above navel).
- **Hip / Seat:** Stand with feet together and measure around the widest point of the buttocks.
- **Inseam:** From the lowest crotch seam down the inside leg seam to the desired hem break at your shoe.
- **Thigh:** Measure around the fullest section of the upper thigh, roughly 1 inch below the crotch seam.

### Footwear Sizing
- Stand barefoot on a sheet of paper against a wall in the evening (feet expand slightly during the day).
- Mark the tip of the longest toe and heel. Measure distance in inches or centimeters.
- Dress shoes (Goodyear welted) typically run 0.5 size larger than cushioned athletic sneakers.

## 2. Garment Dimensions vs Body Dimensions (Ease Allowance)
Garments are cut larger than body measurements to allow mobility:
- **Slim Fit:** +1.5 to +2.5 inches of chest ease over bare body chest.
- **Regular Fit:** +3.0 to +4.0 inches of ease.
- **Relaxed / Oversized:** +5.0 to +8.0 inches of ease.

If your bare body chest is 40 inches:
- Slim shirt garment chest of 42 inches yields a modern tailored fit.
- Garment chest under 41 inches will cause button gaping and armhole constriction.
